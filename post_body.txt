What happens when you let an agentic mesh own your schedule telemetry?

1. Instant DCMA 14-Point Contractual Auditing
The engine parses your P6 SQLite database (.db) or raw .XER in milliseconds. Missing logic, open ends, leads (negative lags), and loose float are detected against Saudi Aramco, NEOM, and US DoD standards before submission.

2. Real-Time EVM & Dynamic S-Curve Generation
DAX measures automatically evaluate Schedule Performance Index (SPI), Cost Performance Index (CPI), and Estimated Duration at Completion (EAC_t). When SPI drops below 1.0, conditional health indicators trigger automated alerts.

3. Zero-Click Headless Chromium Dashboards
On our cloud VM infrastructure, Headless Google Chrome boots without a display server, renders the live executive schedule analytics, and captures pixel-perfect HD snapshots for Telegram (@AzGem_Bot) and executive reporting.

4. One-Click Power BI Desktop Refresh
For management teams who need interactive slicers, the pipeline streams a clean, typed tabular dataset. Pressing "Refresh" in Power BI Desktop instantly updates the entire critical path analysis.
